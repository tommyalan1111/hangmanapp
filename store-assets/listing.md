## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```text
Hangman Movie Trivia is a love letter to the movies — and to the letters that got away.

Pick a category, read the clue, and guess the film, show, actor, actress, character, director, or famous screen family one letter at a time. Six wrong guesses and the credits roll.

18,593 clues. Seven categories. One more reason to argue about movies.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• 18,593 movie and TV clues across Movies, TV Series, Actors, Actresses, Characters, Directors, and Film Families.
• A new Daily Feature every day, worth double points.
• Six takes per round, with a clean, readable keyboard built for one-handed play.
• Hints that reveal a missing letter for 200 points — or watch an optional rewarded video for a free one.
• Progress that stays put: solved puzzles, category completion, win rate, streaks, total points, and recent history.
• An optional one-time Remove Ads upgrade that removes the banner, interstitials, and the rewarded hint.

BUILT FOR FILM LOVERS

Every clue is written for people who quote the credits. Name the gangster epic from a single line of description. Recognise the sitcom from its ensemble. Pick out the director behind the blockbuster. The answers are titles and names you know; the challenge is remembering them before the drawing is finished.

PLAY YOUR WAY

• Offline by default. Puzzles, artwork, sound, hints, and stats work without a connection.
• No accounts, no sign-up, and no server profile.
• No timers. Take the time to think, or use a hint when the title is on the tip of your tongue.
• Sound, haptics, and reduced motion can be switched off in Settings.
• A Daily Feature gives every day a fresh, hand-picked puzzle.

ABOUT ADS

The game is free to play and supported by optional ads. A rewarded video can give you a free hint, and a capped interstitial may appear after a win. The banner stays out of the way during a round. If you prefer an uninterrupted game, the one-time Remove Ads purchase switches off all three ad formats.

No accounts. No data selling. Just you, the clue, and the letters.

Movie titles, show titles, and names are used for trivia purposes and belong to their respective owners.
```
