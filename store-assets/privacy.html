Hangman Movie Trivia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Hangman Movie Trivia is an offline-first movie and TV trivia game. The game itself does not require an account. The Android and iOS builds include optional advertising and an optional one-time purchase, which use network connections and are described below.

What the app stores on your device

The game stores the following locally, using the platform's storage:

  • puzzle progress and the current round
  • solved puzzle IDs
  • win/loss statistics, streaks, points, and recent results
  • your sound, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences
  • today's Daily Feature result
  • whether the ad-free upgrade is active
  • ad frequency-cap timestamps

This gameplay data is not sent to a Hangman Movie Trivia server. There is no account system and no Hangman Movie Trivia analytics backend.

Advertising

The Android and iOS builds use Google AdMob to show:

  • an optional rewarded video that gives the player a free hint
  • a limited interstitial after some completed rounds
  • an adaptive banner at the bottom of the lobby, stats, settings, and help screens

AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • device and app identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system, and app version
  • ad impressions, clicks, and other ad interactions
  • diagnostic and fraud-prevention information

This information is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid traffic, and comply with legal obligations. Hangman Movie Trivia does not receive your name, email address, or payment details from AdMob.

Where required by law, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent before requesting personalized ads. You can review or change those choices from Settings → Ad privacy options. On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads are requested in non-personalized mode.

Google's privacy information is available at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses advertising data

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing on Android or the App Store on iOS. Hangman Movie Trivia does not receive or store your payment card details. The store may provide the app with a purchase confirmation and product entitlement so that ads can be disabled. Restoring a purchase uses the same store account.

Network access

The core game — puzzles, artwork, audio, progress, hints, and stats — works without a network connection. A connection is needed to:

  • load ads
  • show consent/privacy forms
  • complete or restore the Remove Ads purchase

If no ad is available, the game continues normally. No gameplay progress is lost.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — loading ads and completing purchases
  • Billing — the optional Remove Ads purchase
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback, controlled by the Haptics setting

Children's privacy

Hangman Movie Trivia is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is not configured for child-directed treatment. If you intend to publish the app in a children's category, you must change the AdMob settings, store declarations, and consent flow before release.

Your choices

  • Remove Ads — buy the one-time ad-free upgrade in Settings to disable the banner, interstitials, and the rewarded hint.
  • Ad privacy options — review consent choices in Settings where required.
  • iOS tracking — change tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Reset progress — erase local gameplay statistics and history. The ad-free entitlement is preserved.
  • Uninstall — removes the app's local data from the device.

Data retention

Local gameplay data remains on the device until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Ad and purchase data is retained by Google and Apple according to their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included with a new release of the app.
