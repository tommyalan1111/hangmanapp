# Reel Hangman — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

Reel Hangman is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## What the app stores

The game stores the following on your device only, using the platform's local storage:

- puzzle progress and the current round
- solved puzzle IDs
- win/loss statistics, streaks, points, and recent results
- your sound, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences
- today's Daily Feature result

This information never leaves the device. It is not sent to a server, analytics service, advertising network, or third party. There is no account and no login.

## Network access

The Android build does not request the `INTERNET` permission. All puzzles, code, artwork, and sounds are bundled in the app. The web/PWA build precaches its own files with a service worker; it does not contact a backend.

## Advertising and tracking

There are no ads, no trackers, no cookies, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social login.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Permissions

- **Vibration** — used only for optional haptic feedback and controlled by the in-game Haptics setting.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Reset progress** to erase local statistics, solved puzzles, history, and the Daily Feature result. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Sound and motion preferences are kept when using Reset progress.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included with a new release of the app.
